## Week 2: Technology Setup

### Day 8-10: Core Tools Setup

**Priority Order:**
1. **Clay Account** (Start with free, upgrade to Starter $149/month)
   - Learn: Data enrichment, lead discovery, AI research
   - Goal: Build first prospect list of 100 companies

2. **Instantly.ai or Smartlead** ($49-$99/month)
   - Purpose: Email outreach automation
   - Setup: Warm up 3-5 email domains
   - Goal: Create first email sequence template

3. **Make.com or Zapier** (Free tier to start)
   - Purpose: Connect all your tools
   - Setup: Create simple automation workflows
   - Goal: Automate lead data flow

4. **HubSpot (Free tier) or Pipedrive**
   - Purpose: CRM and pipeline tracking
   - Setup: Create deal stages and properties
   - Goal: Track all prospects and clients

### Day 11-14: Build Your First System

**The "Revenue Starter System" Workflow:**
```
1. Define ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
2. Build prospect list in Clay
3. Enrich with data (employees, tech stack, funding)
4. Score leads based on fit
5. Export to CRM
6. Trigger email sequence via Instantly
7. Track responses and meetings booked
8. Follow up via LinkedIn
9. Book discovery calls
10. Close deals
```

**Time to Build:** 10-15 hours
**Goal:** Have a fully functional system running by end of week

## Month 2: Client Acquisition

### Week 5-6: Aggressive Outreach

**Daily Outreach Routine:**
```
Morning (1-2 hours):
• 20 personalized emails to new prospects
• 10 LinkedIn connection requests
• Respond to all inbound messages

Afternoon (1-2 hours):
• Follow up with warm prospects
• Refine messaging based on feedback
• Update prospect lists and scoring

Evening (30 minutes):
• Plan tomorrow's outreach
• Review today's results
• Adjust strategy as needed
```

**Targets:**
- Week 5: Send 100 emails, 50 LinkedIn requests
- Week 6: Send 150 emails, 75 LinkedIn requests
- Goal: Book 20-30 discovery calls

**Outreach Channels Priority:**
1. **Cold Email** (60% of effort) - Highest conversion rate
2. **LinkedIn** (30% of effort) - Good for validation
3. **Referrals** (10% of effort) - Highest quality

### Week 7-8: Sales & Closing

**Discovery Call Goals:**
- 20-30 calls total
- 8-12 second calls (proposals)
- 3-5 clients closed

**Closing Strategy:**
1. **Create Urgency**: Limited spots, special pricing
2. **Social Proof**: Share case studies and testimonials
3. **Risk Reversal**: 30-day guarantee or flexible terms
4. **Clear Next Steps**: Make it easy to say yes

**Pricing for First Clients:**
- Client 1: $4,000/month (get first win)
- Client 2: $5,000/month (prove consistency)
- Client 3: $6,000/month (establish market rate)

**Offer for First Clients:**
"Join as a founding client and get:
• 20% discount for first 3 months
• Priority support and attention
• Help building your case study
• Lock in pricing for 12 months"

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Don't:
1. Try to be everything to everyone
2. Underprice your services
3. Over-deliver in the first month (burnout risk)
4. Ignore client feedback
5. Focus on vanity metrics (emails sent vs. deals closed)
6. Skip proper onboarding
7. Forget to ask for referrals

### ✅ Do:
1. Start with one specific industry
2. Price based on value, not hours
3. Set clear expectations and deliver on them
4. Communicate regularly with clients
5. Focus on business outcomes
6. Document everything as you go
7. Ask for referrals and testimonials
