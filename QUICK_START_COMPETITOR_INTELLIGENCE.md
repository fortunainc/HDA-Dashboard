# Quick Start Guide - Enhanced Competitor Intelligence

## How to Use the Enhanced Competitor Intelligence System

### Step 1: Access the Dashboard
Go to: https://3000-a4698c30-0dee-4ff4-abd1-4c4d9a39bbb1.sandbox-service.public.prod.myninja.ai/competitors

### Step 2: Add a Competitor
1. Click the **"+ Add Competitor"** button
2. Fill in the competitor details:
   - **Name**: Competitor's company name
   - **Website**: Their website URL (e.g., https://competitor.com)
   - **Industry**: Their industry category
3. Click **"Add Competitor"**

### Step 3: Analyze the Competitor
1. Click the **"Analyze"** button on the competitor card
2. Wait 2-3 seconds for the analysis to complete
3. Click **"View Details"** to see the full analysis

### Step 4: Review the Enhanced Data

**What You'll See**:

1. **Traffic Estimates**
   - Low/Mid/High range with confidence level
   - Example: "10,000 - 25,000 visitors/month (Medium confidence)"

2. **Domain Metrics**
   - Domain Age (how long the website has been registered)
   - Domain Authority (0-100 scale)
   - SSL Certificate status

3. **SEO & Social Scores**
   - SEO Score (0-100 scale)
   - Social Score (0-100 scale)
   - Social media follower counts

4. **Technology Stack**
   - CMS (WordPress, HubSpot, etc.)
   - Analytics tools
   - CRM systems
   - Marketing automation platforms

5. **SWOT Analysis**
   - Strengths: What they do well
   - Weaknesses: Areas where they're vulnerable
   - Opportunities: Market gaps you can exploit
   - Threats: External risks

6. **Actionable Recommendations**
   - 5-6 specific strategies to beat them
   - Prioritized by impact

7. **Upsell Opportunities**
   - 4-6 services you can sell
   - Pricing estimates ($2,000-$5,000/month)
   - Priority levels (High/Medium/Low)

8. **Revenue Calculator**
   - Low estimate of potential revenue
   - High estimate of potential revenue
   - Annual potential

### Step 5: Use the Insights for Client Presentations

**Example Script for Client**:

> "Based on our analysis of your competitor [Competitor Name], here's what we found:
>
> **Their Strengths**:
> - Established brand authority (domain age: 8 years)
> - Strong SEO performance (score: 78)
> 
> **Their Weaknesses**:
> - Limited social media engagement (score: 45)
> - No marketing automation system
> - Inconsistent content publishing
> 
> **Opportunities for You**:
> - Launch targeted LinkedIn outreach to decision-makers
> - Implement marketing automation for better lead nurturing
> - Create consistent, high-quality content
> - Increase social media posting frequency
> 
> **Revenue Potential**:
> - Low estimate: $9,500/month
> - High estimate: $15,000/month
> - Annual potential: $147,000/year
>
> Would you like us to help you implement these strategies?"

### Step 6: Present Upsell Opportunities

For each upsell opportunity, explain:
1. **The Problem**: What the competitor is doing wrong
2. **The Solution**: What you'll do for the client
3. **The Value**: How much revenue they'll gain
4. **The Investment**: Your pricing

**Example**:

> "Their SEO score of 65 means you can outrank them in search results. Our SEO Optimization Campaign will:
> - Optimize your website for your target keywords
> - Build quality backlinks
> - Improve your domain authority
> - Increase organic traffic by 50-100%
> 
> Investment: $3,000-$5,000/month
> ROI: 200-400% within 6 months"
