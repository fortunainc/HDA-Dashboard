## 4. Discovery Call Script

### Opening (5 minutes)
```
"Thanks for taking the time to chat today, {first_name}. I'm excited to learn more about {company_name} and see if there's a fit.

Before we dive in, let me give you a quick overview of what I do:
I help B2B companies build AI-powered revenue systems that automatically generate 200-500 qualified leads per month.

My clients typically see their first results within 7 days, and most achieve a 3-5x increase in qualified lead flow within 90 days.

I've got about 30 minutes today - does that work for you?"
```

### Understanding Their Business (10 minutes)
```
"Let's start with some context about your business:

1. Can you tell me a bit about {company_name} and your ideal customer?
2. What's your typical deal size and sales cycle?
3. How are you currently generating leads?
4. What's working well in your current approach?
5. What's your biggest challenge with lead generation right now?"
```

### Goals & Metrics (5 minutes)
```
"Thanks for that context. A few more questions:

1. What are your revenue goals for the next 6-12 months?
2. How many qualified leads do you need per month to hit those goals?
4. What's your current close rate from leads to customers?
5. How much are you currently spending on lead generation?"
```

### The Solution (10 minutes)
```
"Based on what you've shared, here's what I'd recommend:

[Customize based on their needs]

I would build a custom AI-powered revenue system for {company_name} that includes:
• Automated lead discovery and qualification
• Personalized outreach across email and LinkedIn
• Lead scoring and CRM integration
• Weekly reporting on pipeline metrics

This system would typically generate {X} qualified leads per month for companies like yours.

I charge $X,000/month for this complete system, with a 30-day performance guarantee.

Most of my clients see their first qualified leads within 7 days of implementation.

Does this sound like it could be a fit for {company_name}?"
```

### Handling Objections

**"This sounds expensive"**
```
"I understand it's an investment. Let me put it in perspective:

If we generate 200 qualified leads per month at your current close rate of {X}%, that's {Y} deals per month. At your average deal size of $Z, that's $Revenue in additional pipeline each month.

The system pays for itself if you close just {X} deals per month.

And unlike hiring more salespeople, this system works 24/7 without additional overhead."
```

**"We already have marketing/lead generation"**
```
"That's great! What I do is different - I build complete revenue operations systems, not just marketing campaigns.

I focus on:
• Automating the entire lead lifecycle from discovery to qualification
• Ensuring leads are actually sales-ready before they reach your team
• Providing complete visibility into what's driving revenue

Think of it as upgrading your lead generation engine rather than replacing it."
```

**"We need to think about it"**
```
"I completely understand. This is an important decision.

While you're thinking about it, I'd be happy to:
• Send over a case study from a similar company
• Provide a custom estimate of what results you could expect
• Answer any additional questions you have

Would any of that be helpful?"
```

### Next Steps (5 minutes)
```
"Great, I think there's a strong fit here.

Here's what I recommend:
1. I can send over a formal proposal by tomorrow
2. We can start implementation next week
3. You should see your first qualified leads within 7 days

Does that timeline work for you?

I'll need a few things to get started:
• Access to your CRM
• Your current ICP criteria
• Any existing lead lists you have

I'll send over an onboarding checklist as well.

Does this sound good to move forward?"
```

## Quick Tips

**Email Best Practices:**
- Personalize every email (use their name, company, specific details)
- Keep emails under 150 words
- Include one clear call-to-action
- Send emails Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM
- Follow up 3-5 times before giving up

**LinkedIn Best Practices:**
- Connect with 20-30 people per day
- Personalize every connection request
- Share valuable content 2-3x per week
- Engage with prospects' content before pitching
- Use InMail sparingly (only for high-value targets)

**Discovery Call Best Practices:**
- Listen more than you talk (80/20 rule)
- Ask open-ended questions
- Take detailed notes
- Focus on their pain points, not your solution
- Always propose next steps before ending

**Proposal Best Practices:**
- Customize every proposal
- Focus on outcomes and ROI
- Include social proof and case studies
- Make it easy to say yes (clear next steps)
- Follow up within 48 hours

---

Remember: Templates are starting points, not rigid scripts. Always customize based on the specific prospect and situation. The more personalized you can be, the better your results will be.
