## 💬 Sales Scripts and Templates

### **Script 1: Introducing Competitor Intelligence**

"You know, I've been analyzing your top competitors, and I found some interesting opportunities. Their SEO score is 65, while most industry leaders are scoring 80+. This creates a significant gap we can help you close. Would you like me to show you the full analysis and recommendations?"

### **Script 2: Pitching SEO Services**

"Based on my analysis, [Competitor Name] is outranking you on 15 key industry terms. This is costing you an estimated 500 visitors per month, which could be 10-15 qualified leads. I can help you create an SEO campaign targeting these exact keywords. Typical investment is $3,000-$5,000/month, and most clients see results in 60-90 days. Shall we start with a pilot program?"

### **Script 3: Pitching Content Marketing**

"I noticed that [Competitor Name] publishes 4 articles per month, while you're publishing 1. They're capturing more organic traffic and establishing thought leadership. I can help you create a content strategy that not only matches their output but exceeds it in quality. This typically runs $2,500-$4,000/month. Would you like me to create a content calendar for you?"

### **Script 4: Pitching Social Media**

"Their social engagement rate is 3.2%, while yours is 1.1%. This means they're building stronger relationships with your target audience. I can implement a social media management strategy that includes content creation, community management, and paid social campaigns. Investment is $2,000-$3,000/month. Want to see some sample content we could create?"

### **Script 5: Pitching Competitive Intelligence Subscription**

"Competitive intelligence isn't a one-time analysis - it's an ongoing process. I can set up continuous monitoring of [Competitor Name] and provide you with monthly strategic reports that highlight new opportunities, threats, and actions to take. This service is $2,000/month and gives you a significant competitive advantage. Want to try it for 3 months?"
